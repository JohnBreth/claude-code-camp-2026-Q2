# World Notes (tbaMUD)

## Key locations & routes
- Start / login room (new character): The Bakery, Midgaard. Exit s → Main Street.
- Market Square: from Bakery `s, e`. Center of Midgaard. Roads n (temple square), s (common square), e/w (main street).
- Guild of Swordsmen (warrior guild): from Market Square `e, e, s` → Entrance Hall; `e` → Bar of Swordsmen; `s` → Tournament And Practice Yard (guildmaster). Yard has a well leading `d` (unexplored).
- Practice Yard well (`d`): drops into a pitch-black maze (no light). Only the exits south (from the drop room) and north/east/west (from the room south of it) are known; all dark. Dead end without light.
- The Sewer Pipe / The Grand Sewer: under Midgaard, reached through the dark maze below the Practice Yard well. Sewers have small spiders (~34 XP at level 1).
- Grand Sewer: runs south from The Sewer Pipe; side exits east (dark) and south to The South End Of The Grand Pipe. No known exit to the surface yet. Temple Square is NOT reached from here.
- Newbie zone (north of Midgaard, contains the massive minotaur): route NOT yet found. Temple Square is north of Market Square and is the likely lead, but unverified.
- East Gate of Midgaard: Main Street east of the Swordsmen guild entrance, `e`.

## Shops & services
- Armory: south of Main Street, just south of the Bakery (stock unchecked)
- General Store: north side of Main Street, 1 east of Market Square (stock unchecked)
- Pet Shop: south side of Main Street, 1 east of Market Square (stock unchecked)
- Weapon Shop: north side of Main Street, opposite the Swordsmen guild entrance (stock unchecked)
- ATM: Entrance Hall of the Guild of Swordsmen
- Bulletin board: Bar of Swordsmen
